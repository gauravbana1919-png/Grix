# StudyBuddy Sample Knowledge Base

## Purpose
StudyBuddy is a simple study-planning prototype for college students. It helps learners break revision into small, manageable tasks. This document is sample content for testing an AI chatbot knowledge base.

## A basic study plan
1. Understand the basics: read class notes and list the main concepts.
2. Practise key questions: solve examples and mark topics you do not understand.
3. Review weak topics: revisit mistakes and explain concepts in your own words.
4. Test yourself: answer questions without looking at notes.
5. Final revision: review short notes, formulas, definitions and important terms.

## How to plan study time
Choose a realistic daily study duration. Split the time into focused blocks, take short breaks, and include practice questions. Give more time to topics that are difficult or carry more marks. Keep a short buffer for unexpected delays.

## How to prepare for an exam
Check the syllabus first. Use class notes, assignments and previous-year questions when available. Practise writing answers within a time limit. On the final day, revise key points and sleep well instead of trying to learn everything at the last minute.

## Chatbot response rules
Answer questions using the information in this document. If the answer is not present, say that the knowledge base does not contain enough information. Do not invent facts, marks, deadlines, or university rules. Keep answers clear and student-friendly.
